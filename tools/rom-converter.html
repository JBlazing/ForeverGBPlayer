GB ROM Converter

Turns Game Boy .gb and Game Boy Color .gbc ROMs into ROM pack addons for ForeverGBPlayer, zipped and ready to extract into your AddOns folder. Each ROM becomes its own small addon that registers itself with ForeverGBPlayer when WoW starts. Conversion runs in this tab: your ROMs are never uploaded.

Only use ROMs you legally own. That means backups you made yourself from your own cartridges. Don't download copies of games you don't own. Forever GB Player doesn't include any games.

2 · Check the conversion

Nothing yet. Each ROM you add shows up here with its cartridge header: title, mapper, Color support, save RAM and checksums. The name field is what you type after /fgb play.

3 · Download and install

Add a ROM to enable the download.
  1. Open the zip (double-click it) and drag the folder inside into your WoW AddOns folder, the same folder that has ForeverGBPlayer in it. Each game is its own folder:
(add a ROM to see the folders)
  1. Don't use "Extract All". It puts the games one folder too deep, and WoW won't find them.
  2. Restart WoW. A /reload doesn't pick up new addon folders. Each game shows in the AddOns list as GB ROM: <title>, and you can turn each one on or off there.
  3. In game, type /fgb play <name>. /fgb roms lists your games.
